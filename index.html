Walking around

Auto tour: tap ▶ Auto tour (or press T) and it walks you through everything by itself. Go to… walks you to one place. Drag to look around while it walks; walking yourself stops it.
Computer: W A S D or arrow keys to walk, hold Shift to go faster, drag to look around, M for the map.
Phone: left circle to walk, drag anywhere else to look.
Map: drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom, tap the ground to move there.
Day / Night: Auto follows Dubai time (night 7 PM – 6 AM). Tap to switch; the team jumps to what they do at that time.
Weather: Auto changes every few minutes (sunny, cloudy, rain). Tap to choose. In the rain the team opens umbrellas.
Team: everyone lives on Dubai time: work, meals, free time, sleep (top bunks first). Tap a person to see who they are, or open Team to walk to them.
Fountains: a show every half hour; tap the lake to start one.
